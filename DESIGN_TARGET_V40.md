# V40 visual parity target — enlarged Domprojekt Anamarija

## Source of truth

This project is a **premium, accessible enlargement of the Domprojekt Anamarija**, not a literal copy of the stock catalogue house and not a generic luxury villa.

- Base architectural identity: Domprojekt Anamarija low-energy single-storey house.
- Working design target: approximately **200 m² internal area**.
- The original room zoning and Anamarija character remain the starting point, while circulation, principal rooms, glazing and accessibility are enlarged.
- The approved reference boards supplied for the project govern aesthetic intent: warm ivory/plaster, Calacatta-style stone, warm natural oak, restrained bronze/dark aluminium, concealed warm lighting, panoramic glazing and premium landscaped indoor/outdoor living.
- This repository remains a visual design-development model until exact architect/Domprojekt drawings replace all provisional dimensions.

## Architectural targets

1. Preserve the recognisable single-storey Anamarija massing and zoning rather than drifting into an unrelated villa typology.
2. Target approximately 200 m² by allocating additional area deliberately to the living zone, circulation/accessibility, master accommodation, bathrooms, storage/utility and transitions rather than uniformly scaling every room.
3. General finished ceiling target: approximately 2.8–3.0 m.
4. Main living zone may rise to approximately 4.0 m where compatible with the final roof/structure.
5. Tall internal doors: target approximately 2.2–2.3 m.
6. Principal accessible circulation: target approximately 1.5 m where practical, with step-free routes and generous turning areas.
7. Large-format panoramic glazing should visually connect living/dining/kitchen to terrace, pool and garden.
8. Terrace, garden and 8 × 4 m pool are part of the finished home experience, not secondary scenery.

## Hero route

V40 prioritises visual parity along this sequence:

**Entrance → hallway → living room → kitchen / dining → panoramic glazing → terrace → pool**

This route must receive the highest-quality geometry, production assets, PBR surfaces, architectural lighting, reflections and planting before secondary rooms are refined to the same standard.

## Material language

- Main circulation / open-plan floor: large-format polished or honed Calacatta-style porcelain/stone.
- Bedrooms and selected private areas: warm natural oak / herringbone.
- Walls: warm ivory / warm-white plaster, low sheen.
- Joinery: warm oak and complementary warm neutral cabinetry.
- Metal/frame accents: dark bronze / dark aluminium, restrained rather than black-heavy.
- Soft furnishings: ivory, oatmeal, warm greige and muted natural accents.
- Lighting: warm concealed coves, downlights and decorative pendants balanced against neutral daylight.

## Walkthrough experience target

The visual references define the experience as well as the interior design. The live viewer should progressively include:

- first-person walking navigation;
- wheelchair eye-height/navigation mode;
- room jump navigation;
- daytime / evening / night presentation states;
- fullscreen presentation;
- guided hero-route presentation;
- floor-plan/minimap context once it can be generated from the actual project geometry;
- hotspot and thumbnail presentation only when backed by real scene viewpoints/renders.

Do not introduce decorative UI elements that imply functionality which is not actually implemented.

## V40 acceptance criteria

V40 is a **visual parity milestone**, not a claim of construction-document accuracy. It is successful when:

1. The hero route reads as the same premium home shown in the approved boards at normal walkthrough distance.
2. Architectural scale, ceiling height, door height, glazing proportions and accessible clearances feel intentional and consistent.
3. Major visible furniture is production-quality GLB/glTF where available; procedural placeholders are retained only where necessary and should not dominate hero views.
4. Main visible surfaces use physically plausible PBR response with safe fallbacks.
5. Day/evening/night states preserve material readability and warm architectural lighting.
6. Navigation UI is presentation-quality and functional on desktop and touch devices.
7. Changes do not knowingly reduce the accessibility envelope or break collision/navigation QA.

## Construction-status warning

Nothing in V40 supersedes final dimensioned architect, Domprojekt, structural, MEP, fire-safety or accessibility documentation. Exact wall positions, spans, glazing systems, thresholds, door clear openings, drainage, services and furniture clearances remain subject to final professional drawings and approvals.
