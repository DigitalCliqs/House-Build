# V44 environment realism pass

This pass adds stable local image-based-lighting behaviour without requiring a bundled HDR file.

- Three.js RoomEnvironment is converted through PMREM and assigned as the scene environment
- reflective response is increased on hero-route physical materials
- subtle hemispheric fill preserves detail in the hall and ceiling recesses
- a soft rear-glazing bounce light improves the open-plan daylight read
- remote CC0 surface textures remain optional fallbacks rather than a hard dependency

The walkthrough remains a visual design-development model pending final product models, architect dimensions and final material selections.
